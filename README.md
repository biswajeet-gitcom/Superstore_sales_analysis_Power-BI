## `$ cat project.md`

**Superstore Global Sales Analytics** is an interactive business intelligence project built with **Microsoft Power BI** to analyse global retail sales, profitability, customers, products and shipping performance between **2011 and 2014**.

The project transforms transactional Superstore data into an executive-level analytical dashboard that helps identify sales trends, profitable categories, underperforming products, geographic opportunities and discount-related profitability risks.

The analysis covers **51,290 order lines across 147 countries**, with a focus on turning raw transactional data into concise business insights.

---

## `$ ./analysis --questions`

The project explores the following business questions:

- How did sales and profit change between 2011 and 2014?
- Which product categories contribute most to revenue?
- Which sub-categories generate or destroy profit?
- Which countries contribute the highest sales?
- Which customer segments drive the business?
- How do global markets compare in sales and profitability?
- Which shipping modes dominate order volume?
- How do discounts affect profitability?
- Where are the major opportunities and risks in the business?

## `$ ./insights --summary`

### 📈 Sales Growth

Sales increased from **$2.26M in 2011** to **$4.30M in 2014**, representing approximately **90% growth** across the four-year period.

2014 alone contributed approximately **26% more sales than 2013**.

---

### 💻 Category Performance

Technology generated the largest share of sales at approximately **37.5%**, followed by:

| Category | Sales Share |
|---|---:|
| Technology | **37.53%** |
| Furniture | **32.52%** |
| Office Supplies | **29.96%** |

Technology also delivers the strongest category margin at approximately **14%**.

---

### ⚠️ Profitability Risk

**Tables** is the major loss-making sub-category, producing approximately **-$64K** in profit.

The dashboard highlights this as an important profitability issue because Tables also carries a high average discount of approximately **29%**.

---

### 🎯 Discount Impact

Orders receiving **30% or more discount** operate at approximately **-46.8% margin**.

This indicates that aggressive discounting can significantly reduce or eliminate profitability even when order volume remains strong.

## `$ ./profitability --subcategories`

The dashboard analyses profitability across product sub-categories.

### Strongest contributors

- **Copiers — $259K**
- **Phones — $217K**
- **Bookcases — $162K**
- **Chairs — $142K**
- **Appliances — $142K**
- **Accessories — $130K**

### Key concern

- **Tables — -$64K**

This comparison helps identify where revenue translates into healthy profit and where discounting or pricing strategy may require attention.

---

## `$ ./markets --compare`

The dashboard compares **Sales vs Profit** across:

`APAC · EU · US · LATAM · EMEA · Africa · Canada`

This market-level view provides a broader geographic perspective beyond individual countries and helps identify regions where revenue and profitability behave differently.

## `$ ./yearly-performance`

The four-year trend shows consistent growth in both **sales and profit**, with 2014 delivering the strongest overall performance.
